# MC-102* sensitivity

Generated by `evaluation/prediction/scripts/mc102_sensitivity.py`.

`MC-102*` is excluded from the evaluation set as a historical decision with no surviving rationale (entry `X05` in `scope_exclusions.json`). It is the only exclusion in that list that no rule supports and none should: a conventional cast Ni--Cr--Mo--Nb alloy of the 625 family, nearest training neighbour Cast Alloy 625 at d = 5.13, which places it in the **FAR stratum** -- the one the headline agent claim rests on.

It is kept out only so the committed results stay reproducible. This is what that costs. Deterministic arms only; no shipped table changes.

## Reading

Largest single movement: **EL on FAR for ML+physics+KG**, 10.89 → 11.38 (+4.4%).

Yield strength on FAR, the load-bearing comparison, moves -1.5% for ML-only, -1.5% for ML+physics, -1.5% for ML+physics+KG.

Elongation is the outlier and is worth stating plainly: the arms predict MC-102* at roughly 30% elongation against a measured 5% at room temperature, so including it moves EL by up to 4.4%. That is one alloy exposing a known weakness -- ductility of cast alloys is the property every arm predicts worst -- not a new one.

The exclusion is disclosed rather than silent, and re-admitting MC-102* is recorded in `scope_exclusions.json` as a decision for the next results freeze.
